## Steps

1. **Create a Grid node.** Right-click the canvas → Add Node → Grid.
2. **Add Mountain.** Wire grid1 into Mountain. Adjust Height and Frequency.
3. **Scatter points.** Add Scatter and connect Mountain's output.
4. **Inspect attributes.** Open Spreadsheet → Points to see @P, @N live.
